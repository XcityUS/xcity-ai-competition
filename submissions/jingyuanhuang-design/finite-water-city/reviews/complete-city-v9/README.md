# AGUA FINITA V9 / planning review

Read the first sheet, `00-whole-site-regions.png`, before the urban enlargement. Six gross strategic regions partition the entire competition site. Region geometry and programme are in `geometry/planning_regions.geojson` and `regional-programme.json`. They are proposed planning demarcations, not sourced legal zones. Full-site roads remain corridor studies, not acquired rights.

## Drawings and evidence

- `agua-finita-v9-complete-city-review.pdf`: 15 A3 pages; regional plan first, territorial context, full planned R/C town, six distinct city themes, overall orthographic axonometric, A03/B03/C03 plans and illustrative sections, phase map/table and resources.
- `agua-finita-v9-a0-planning-review.pdf`: whole-site regions, urban plan, massing, separate productive inset and phase map/account.
- `geometry/`: EPSG:4326 GeoJSON; design calculations use UTM19S (EPSG:32719). Stable building/unit/phase IDs connect drawings to counts.
- `source-freeze.json`: input fingerprints. `view-registry.json`: figure extents, projection, phases and diagrammatic-view disclosures.
- `qa.json`: source/fixed/channel screening, footprint overlap, buffered road-reserve conflicts and district land accounting. It is internal geometric QA, not organiser design assessment.
- `metrics.json`, `phasing_programme.json`, `assumptions.json`: design scenarios and conditional releases. Employment is programme capacity, not signed jobs. Resident counts exclude roster beds.

Run from repository root: `../venv312/bin/python submissions/jingyuanhuang-design/finite-water-city/tools/complete_city_v9.py`.

## Limits before final submission

All drawings are original source-based vector diagrams, not renderer screenshots or claimed photorealistic renderings. The axonometric uses a flat analytical datum and heights x3, declared on-sheet. No images are reused from the superseded submission. New finished node aerial/eye-level perspectives, complete whole-territory thematic depth, regional alternatives, transport/civil and environmental validation, verified water balance including fixed pilots, and funded service/livelihood evidence remain outstanding. The town-wide network test uses only released gross cells at each phase and measures block gate to school gate, not individual dwellings or safe crossings.

Source terrain screening does not establish statutory no-build land. Regional economic/land-use roles are not installations or irrigation commitments. Both fixed pilots remain unchanged. The region boundary proposal must be reviewed on its own merits before generating presentation renders or updating the public gallery.
