## Why the old scheme is being rebuilt

1. Its roughly 40.6 km² master-study extent was not evidence that all that land had complete urban design. The small built neighbourhood and wider reservations were too easily confused.
2. Separate town/production reserves were not backed by a complete zone/phase construction programme. Earlier capacity and jobs did not explain every later settlement.
3. Compute proximity, heat receiver and the resource consequences of adjacency were insufficiently established.
4. Finished local images were allowed to stand for a larger design extent; their geometry/camera agreement needs renewed review.
5. Flood-screening extent is neither a lake nor a surveyed no-build designation.

## Candidate revision

Complete public-service plots and a continuous street/civic network are drawn separately from the production plot. A plot reservation does not prove an operating school or clinic.
- Compute/repair is a separate 0.42 km² plot with three 2 MW hall scenarios. Distances and routes are computed from the candidate geometry. No speculative heat reuse or inherited hourly energy-matching result is credited.
- A03 is the climate commons; B03 the separate compute/repair campus; C03 a wash-park extent containing an actual supplied channel line. None is a proxy for the whole-site design.
- New-development water demand is an explicit scenario. Fixed-pilot/living-quarter demand remains unknown and must be added before any source-capacity conclusion. No secured source is asserted.
