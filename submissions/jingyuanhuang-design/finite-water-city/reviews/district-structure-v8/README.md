# V8: residential districts and productive precincts

## Human direction and the design correction

The human rejected a repeated micro-city pattern: a small housing layout with every function inserted around it. Residential land should form a continuous district; compute campuses need their own siting and operating conditions. The complete-first-phase requirement does not put every urban function inside every neighbourhood.

V8 therefore begins with the whole-site system and districts. It does not enlarge the same sixteen-block cartoon or distribute more facilities around it. V7's dwelling quota, all-functions layout and unsupported three-town population ledger are withdrawn.

## What has actually been designed in this review

- **R:** a source-clipped, continuous residential planning envelope, divided into connected south/north release precincts. Everyday shops, schools, local care and neighbourhood public space stay within residential catchments. A few backbone streets and walks identify the structure; blocks, houses, doors and final service capacities have not been drawn yet.
- **C:** a compact mixed-use town-centre transition on the southern arrival edge of R. Its gross reserve is distinct from the initial 12 ha activity core. It is not a 60 ha public plaza or a requirement to construct all town-level facilities at once.
- **P:** a separate compute/repair/maintenance planning precinct south of R. Its boundary is 3.4 km from R's boundary. This is a spatial proposal, not an acoustic or statutory clearance. Actual platforms need further terrain and civil design.
- **F/A/S:** measured corridor alternatives for freight, passenger arrival and worker shuttle. Freight is checked against both R and C exclusions. The start is on the actual western boundary at the chosen latitude, not a convenient arbitrary point inside the site. The regional RN40 junction remains unselected and external rights are not secured.
- **L:** an explicit connection study to the separate fixed living-quarter outline. It does not bring that quarter into a walkable service catchment or invent its housing capacity.
- **Whole site:** primary housing is distinct from distributed compute, agriculture, energy and the conditional eastern gateway. A northern farm-service option is not automatically a town. Both fixed pilot parts are retained unchanged.
- **Nodes:** A03 belongs to the initial town-centre core; B03 to P; C03 is a separate wash-park study, with visitor works in Phase 2 and conveyance protection from the outset. Their detail extents are independent.

## Evidence, measures and limits

`qa.json` records source DEM cell distributions, land accounts, actual route lengths, overlap/exclusion checks and current source revision. Full-cell distributions replace the earlier centre-cell terrain shortcut. Some P land is not flat: the planning envelope is not proof that every platform can be built. Flood screening is not a lake or a hydraulic safety certificate. Added-seam land is unclassified hydrologically.

PASS at this stage means only the reported geometric tests: district non-overlap and containment, fixed-pilot avoidance, complete whole-site role coverage, the freight exclusion and route endpoint connections. It does not mean the design is complete, the corridors engineered, or the programme financed and permitted.

WARNINGS: regional access, detailed channel crossings, supply rights, reliable source yield/quality, pilot and existing-quarter demand/stock, power, noise/dust, seismic/civil engineering, school demographics, employment demand and local walking/service capacity remain to verify. A 300 m candidate route is a corridor study, not a road centreline suitable for construction.

REQUIRED DESIGN WORK: iterate actual housing blocks and net land with service catchments and capacities; locate production platforms within P; resolve all first works; establish occupancy/jobs scenarios and their resources; then produce geometry-linked Phase 1/2/3 construction programmes and new matching controls/finished imagery. Do not reuse old images to represent relocated districts.

DO NOT CHANGE: current issued boundary, either fixed pilot, forage grass/jojoba under the arrays, the channel construction rule, English final deliverables, or the human's requirement for continuous residential districts and independently justified production locations.
