# City-design method and review basis / 城市设计方法与复核依据

Checked against official sources on 27 October 2026. These are methodological references, not a claim that English or Chinese statutory procedures apply to the Argentine site. The six thematic requirements are XCITY's competition specification; the sources do not prescribe that exact six-sheet package.

## Official references

- [England: National Model Design Code, Part 1, §2.C Master planning, paragraphs 42–46 and Figure 11](https://www.gov.uk/government/publications/national-model-design-code/national-model-design-code-part-1-the-coding-process-html-accessible-version#c-master-planning). Basis: relate the masterplan's streets, area types, centres, landscape and development parameters before applying detailed guidance.
- [England: National Model Design Code, Part 2, M.1.iii Street hierarchy; Built Form; Public Space; Use](https://www.gov.uk/government/publications/national-model-design-code/national-model-design-code-part-2-guidance-notes-html-accessible-version). Basis: relate street roles to movement and place, and coordinate development form, use and public space.
- [China: Measures for Urban Design Administration, Articles 3, 8 and 10](https://www.mohurd.gov.cn/gongkai/zhengce/gzk/art/2022/art_17337_763796.html). Basis: coordinate overall plan and three-dimensional form, natural landscape and public-space systems, then resolve spatial scale and built form in detailed areas.

## How XCITY applies the method

| Decision | Required design evidence | Review question |
|---|---|---|
| Define the city structure and districts | Named/numbered districts, centres, principal links and coherent roles on the complete layout | Do the districts form a city or functioning settlement system, rather than isolated coloured areas? |
| Coordinate streets and programme | Main arrivals, primary/secondary streets, district connections, public/service/freight movement and functional allocations | Are uses reachable and supported by appropriate streets and services? |
| Allocate land and capacities | Readable land units, use legend, mixing convention, area/capacity table and district identifiers | Do the land, population, employment and service accounts refer to the same spatial units? |
| Organise landscape and public life | Terrain/channel response, linked open space, civic anchors and service access | Does the arrangement work under the actual water, heat, wind and access constraints? |
| Resolve spatial form and nodes | Heights/massing, decisive street sections and detailed node plans tied to overall decisions | Do the detailed places support the city structure? |
| Explain the whole and its construction | Overall axonometric/birdseye from the same layout, phase map and matching programme | Can a reviewer understand the complete scheme, its spatial character and delivery sequence? |

District boundaries, functional allocations, land-use units and construction phases answer different questions. Do not silently use one set of coloured polygons as all four. They may share boundaries where justified; show the relationship through stable district IDs, legends and account tables. Colour blocks are appropriate analytical tools when they identify meaningful spatial units and relationships. They are not evidence of design by themselves. At city scale, establish principal/secondary streets and a legible district/block structure; resolve local streets and individual buildings in the relevant enlargements. Do not pretend that the entire site needs construction-document detail.

The sequence is iterative: site and programme → city structure and coordinated systems → urban design and nodes → overall spatial communication and implementation checks. The overall spatial view is derived from the design, not used to invent its geometry. A render-only submission omits required planning evidence even if its images are attractive. The checker validates references and encoded relationships; reviewers must inspect whether the diagrams make useful spatial decisions. AI Agent booklets have no fixed page count.

## 中文说明

以上采用官方发布的方法依据：英国《National Model Design Code》的总体布局、路网层级、建筑形态、公共空间及用途指导；中国《城市设计管理办法》第3、8、10条关于整体平面、立体空间、自然格局、公共空间和重点地区尺度的要求。它们用于组织本竞赛的设计工作，不把外国法定审批流程当成本项目的既定条件；六类专题是XCITY结合本次任务制定的成果要求，不声称某份规范规定必须画这六张。

先通过场地与功能研究建立城市结构和分区，同时协调道路、生态、公服、用地及容量。分区图应有明确边界、名称／编号和角色；功能分区、土地利用单元、城市地区边界、建设分期不是同一个概念，不能用同一组色块不加解释地代替。可重合，但须说明关系，并与面积、人口、岗位和设施表对应。

城市级先画清主要与次要路网、出入口、中心和地区／街块结构；局部放大再深化支路、建筑和街道断面，不要求全场地达到逐栋施工图细度。色块可以帮助理解，但必须表达有用的空间判断。随后深化城市设计和三个节点，再由同一布局制作整体轴测／大鸟瞰并核对分期。只有渲染、没有必需规划证据，不能认定完成。AI Agent图册不设固定页数。
