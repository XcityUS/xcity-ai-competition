# Whitepaper V7: planning use and updates

Source reviewed: owner-supplied `XCITY白皮书V7(1).pdf`, October 2026, pages 4-8 and 17-18. This digest does not republish the source PDF or certify its projections.

| Topic | Retain as design foundation | Reconcile before using as a current constraint |
|---|---|---|
| Five functions, page 5 | Energy/compute 60, agriculture 30, living 5, industry 3, trade/port 2 as the common approximate starting balance; justified exploration encouraged | Distinguish original functional-region shares from current overlapping system weights; separate exclusive land and phased works |
| Scope, page 4 | Energy-led territorial ambition | About 400,000 ha is the older project scope; current competition geometry is approximately 1,659 km2, not that entire project |
| Energy/compute, pages 4, 7-8 | Solar power supports compute and productive work | 600 GW is a long-term stated ambition, not installed capacity or an instruction for this site. Verify land intensity, hourly power, grid/storage, IT/facility load and cooling |
| Agriculture, pages 5, 17 | Productive cultivation and processing chain | Large irrigated agriculture and named rivers/aquifers do not prove local water rights or annual supply. Current confirmed under-panel programme is forage + jojoba; other crops are separate optional trials subject to suitability |
| Living, pages 6, 18 | Housing with schools, health, commerce, civic life and access to work | A million residents is a long-term stated goal, not a current compulsory population. Water-surrounded housing is a vision, not verified permanent water or permission to create ornamental lakes |
| Industry/logistics, pages 5, 17 | Grouped production, repair, agricultural processing and freight connections | Do not treat the whole coloured region as a commissioned industrial/free-port estate. Justify throughput, facility footprint, access, land reserve and timing |
| Access and governance, pages 4-5, 17 | Study regional trade and future connections | Tunnel/rail/port/customs/financial status remains contingent; use current issued access evidence and do not assume legal approvals or old west-side port placement |

Related owner-supplied notes reviewed: `XCITY 虚拟城市农光结合板块的分析和建议.docx`, `XCITY虚拟城市综合农业系统构架.docx`, `Xcity农业品类.docx`, `XCITY_光伏切入_工作笔记.docx` and the initial concept-planning note. They offer programme hypotheses and operating questions, not universal yields/water/clearance standards. Wider crop/livestock suggestions do not override the owner's confirmed forage/jojoba programme. Conflicting water descriptions, broad climate claims, livestock performance and particular 100-ha prototype percentages require local evidence; they are not silently adopted into the competition.

Changes made: preserve current boundary and pilots; clarify the reference-versus-land distinction; require reasons for scale/departure; add an advisory geometry/programme script and link it from the agent workflow. No new fixed numerical land quota, demand assumption or statutory approval is introduced. Existing proposal distributions are not automatically redesigned or approved by this clarification.
