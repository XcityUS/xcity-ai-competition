# 气候 · Climate

主办方**不提供**气候时间序列。下面写清楚场地的气候条件对设计意味着什么、数据去哪里取、怎么声明。
你用了哪一份数据、哪一段年份、哪个站点或格点，写进 `assumptions.json` 与 `sources.json`。**主办方没有提供的数据，不会成为扣分理由。**

The organiser **does not supply** a climate series. Below: what the climate means for design, where to get data, and how to declare it. Write the dataset, years and station or grid cell you used into `assumptions.json` and `sources.json`. **A gap in the data the organiser supplied is never a reason to mark an entry down.**

## 设计上必须回应的条件 · Conditions a scheme must answer

| 条件 Condition | 对设计意味着什么 What it means for design |
|---|---|
| 干旱 · Arid | 降水极少、蒸发远大于降水。所有绿化与水面都要进水平衡。 · Rain is scarce and evaporation far exceeds it; every planted area and water surface enters the water balance. |
| 强日照 · Strong sun | 光伏的基础；同时遮荫决定人能不能出门。 · The basis of the solar resource — and shade decides whether people can go outside. |
| 日较差大 · Large daily range | 夏季白天酷热、冬季夜间接近冰点。热惰性与夜间通风是可用的手段。 · Very hot summer days, near-freezing winter nights; thermal mass and night ventilation are available tools. |
| Zonda 焚风 · The Zonda | 干、热、强、带尘的下坡风，从西侧安第斯山来。影响光伏积尘与清洗用水、风冷进风、室外舒适度、街道朝向。方案要说明对它的姿态：挡、导，还是让开。 · A dry, hot, strong, dust-laden downslope wind from the Andes to the west. It affects panel soiling and cleaning water, air-cooled intakes, outdoor comfort and street orientation. State the scheme's stance: block, channel, or step aside. |
| 暴雨与干河床行洪 · Storms and flash floods | 一年大部分时间沟道是干的，上游暴雨可在很短时间内行洪。干河床上不得布置永久建设。 · Channels are dry most of the year; an upstream storm can send a flood down them within a short time. No permanent construction on a dry channel. |
| 暗夜 · Dark sky | 圣胡安省内有天文观测设施。方案须给出夜间照明与光污染控制策略；与观测设施的距离及是否存在相关法规，主办方未核实，须自行声明假设。 · San Juan province hosts astronomical observatories. Give a night-lighting and light-pollution strategy; distance to the observatories and whether a regulation applies are unverified by the organiser — declare your assumption. |
