# 试点区 · Pilot area

13 号地块内的三片光伏农业区（01–03），外轮廓合计 13.9 km²，每片 1,000 组阵列。**XCITY 自己最先建设，不在设计范围内。**

Three agrivoltaic fields (01–03) inside parcel 13, 13.9 km² of outline, 1,000 arrays each. **XCITY builds them first itself; they are not open to design.**

## 方案要遵守的 · What a scheme must respect

- 不得在试点区内布置任何建设，也不得改动它的排布。自检脚本会检查你的几何是否压到试点区。
- 板下是已经在运行的共生系统：霍霍巴、迷迭香、饲用仙人掌与羊驼。排布不得改动。
- 开心果、橄榄、葡萄等高株与藤本作物不能进入板阵；它们放在哪里、种多少，由方案提出。
- 试点区是已经存在的东西。城市要长在它旁边：两者之间那条边怎么组织、随城市成长怎么移动，是方案要回答的。

- Nothing may be built inside the pilot area and its layout may not change. The self-check tests your geometry against it.
- Under the arrays is a symbiotic system already running: jojoba, rosemary, forage cactus and alpaca. Its layout is fixed.
- Tall and climbing crops — pistachio, olive, vine — cannot enter the arrays; where they go and how much is for the scheme to propose.
- The pilot area already exists. The city grows beside it: how that edge is organised, and how it moves as the city grows, is the scheme's to answer.
